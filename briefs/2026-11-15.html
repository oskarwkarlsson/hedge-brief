L/S

Viking says its long-short spread is cracking

Andreas Halvorsen told investors the long-short spread, Viking's "Holy Grail" KPI, was just 0.3% in Q3 and the book has been "too balanced"; PMs are being pushed to bet bigger, with pay adjusted. The $35bn fund lost 1.2% in October and is up 1% this year.
