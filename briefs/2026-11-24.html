Nvidia reinstates Huang; S&P +4.73%, Nasdaq +6.51%, best day since April 2025

Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO (8-K dated Nov 22), citing input from senior US government officials; the stock closed +31.48% at $165.05, about $950bn of market value in a day.
